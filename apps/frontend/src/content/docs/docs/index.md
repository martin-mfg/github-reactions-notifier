---
title: Overview
description: What GitHub Reactions Notifier does.
---

GitHub Reactions Notifier tells you when someone reacts to your issues, pull requests and comments on GitHub.

GitHub itself does not notify you about reactions. Once you sign in, this service watches the content you authored and records new reactions for you.

You can choose between two options when you sign in:

- **Get notified about reactions:** a one-time summary of the reactions your content has received so far, then a notification for every new reaction.
- **Just send me a one-time summary:** the summary only, nothing after that.

You can switch between them at any time. Turning notifications on, or asking for the summary again after it arrived, rescans your history and posts a fresh summary.
